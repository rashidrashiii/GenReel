BEATS
- A beat is 1 to 3 seconds: one idea, 3 to 9 spoken words (Malayalam and Tamil: 3 to 7). Read one after another, all "narration" parts must sound like natural, complete sentences (a sentence may run across two or three beats). The narration is also the caption, so never repeat it elsewhere.

- The last beat is the closing line: for a business or event, what to do now (visit, call, order, register, message); for a wish, tip or personal video, a warm sign-off. WHAT IS SHOWN
